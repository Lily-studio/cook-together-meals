# Homepage category card upgrade

## What will change
- Replace the current three-card feature presentation with five spacious, easy-to-scan rectangular category cards: Meal Planning, Recipes / Discover, Shopping / Grocery, Saved Meals / Meal Book, and Preferences.
- Give each card a cohesive miniature 3D cooking illustration with smooth matte materials, soft lighting, gentle depth, and restrained shadows.
- Preserve the established homepage palette, typography, spacing rhythm, rounded corners, and premium Lily visual language.
- Keep motion subtle and accessible, with a small lift/depth response and reduced-motion support.

## What will stay unchanged
- All navigation, sign-in behavior, links, branding, application logic, and authenticated screens.
- The cards remain simple visual summaries rather than adding new controls or workflows.

## Technical details
- Keep all new presentation styles isolated to the public landing page.
- Store the new visual assets locally with the app and use responsive image sizing.
- Verify the section at desktop and mobile widths, including text fit, card spacing, loading, and console/build status.
